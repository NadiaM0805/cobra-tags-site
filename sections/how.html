Quick Start – Cobra Tag Pairing

Unbox your Cobra Tag and remove the battery tab.

Power ON: Press and hold the button for 3 seconds until you hear "Ring Ring."

Power OFF: Press and hold for 3 seconds again — you'll hear 2 beeps.

Reset (before pairing a new device):

  • Press twice (you'll hear 1 beep).
  • Then press 4 times rapidly and hold on the last press until you hear "Ring Ring."
  • Your tag is now reset and ready to pair.

Choose platform:

  • iPhone: Open Find My → Items → + → Other Item → follow prompts.
  • Android: Open Find Hub → Items → Add → follow prompts.

Name your tag (e.g., Keys, Bike, Wallet, Luggage).

Attach using the keyring or stick-on card.

💡 If your Cobra Tag isn't detected, perform a reset and try pairing again. Full setup video and printed guide are included in the box.
