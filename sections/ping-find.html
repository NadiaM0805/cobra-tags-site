Ping it. Find it. Keep it safe.

Losing something you care about doesn't have to be stressful. With your Cobra Tag, simply play a sound from your phone and follow the tone — no arrow guidance needed. Whether it's your keys, bike, or even your pet, the app makes sure you always know where your things are. When the CR2032 battery starts running low, you'll get a notification in the app so you can easily replace it and stay connected.
